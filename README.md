# WEBSITETAMS — THE AAMIR SHEIKH

Personal website of THE AAMIR SHEIKH, live at https://theaamirsheikh.com (Cloudflare Pages, project `websitetams`).

- Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. No framework, no build step on the server.
- **All pages are generated** by `_build/build.py` → run `python3 _build/build.py` from the repo root after editing.
- Translations (9 languages: English, Deutsch, Français, 日本語, Español, Русский, العربية, 中文, 한국어) live in `_build/i18n.py`, `i18n_more.py`, `i18n_cjk.py`, and `i18n_p0.py` plus the step files `i18n_acct.py`, `i18n_admin.py`, `i18n_shop.py`, `i18n_privacy.py`, `i18n_polish.py` (newest strings, one row per key). The build writes one file per language to `assets/js/i18n/` (`de.js`, `fr.js` …); a page loads only the visitor's language, English needs none.
- Three interfaces, each with its own music: **Riot** (electric guitar), **Noir** (smooth jazz), **Village** (ambient village). Visitors can link or unlink music from the interface in Settings (S key).
- Accessibility panel: colour-vision modes, high contrast, brightness, text size, reduced motion, underline links, read-aloud.
- Order & feedback form (`order.html`) sends to WhatsApp (wa.me prefill) and Gmail (FormSubmit — confirm the activation email the first time).
- Video: short films are bit-exact copies of the originals (or CRF 16 when over 24 MB). **Money Follows (v1)** streams as HLS from `assets/media/money-follows/` — the 4K rung is the original file, untouched, cut into 2.5 s chunks (Cloudflare Pages caps files at 25 MB).
- Music: Pixabay (Pixabay Content License). Photos: Unsplash. Icons: Lucide (ISC). Characters in `assets/img/chars/` are original SVG illustrations.
- Raw sources stay on the computer in `_source_media/` (git-ignored).
- Navigation (v3.4): **Home · Work · About · Services · Contact**. About = Story, Struggles, Skills, Inspirations (sub-nav on those pages); Contact = `order.html`. `/about` and `/contact` redirect there.
- Intro: 3 seconds, then continues quietly; Skip is visible from the first frame. Sound (music and click sounds) is off until the visitor turns it on. The intro can be switched off in Settings.
- Lighthouse game: `404.html` and `play.html` (footer + menu link). The dark hides six works; the light follows the mouse, finger or arrow keys. Edit the six works in `LH_WORKS` in `_build/build.py`.
- Backend (accounts and payments, in progress): Cloudflare Pages Functions in `functions/api/` and a D1 database. The map, the setup commands and the list of secrets are in `_db/README.md`. Secrets live only in Cloudflare, never in this repo.
- Account page (`account.html`): sign in with Google or a 6-digit code by email, see your own orders, sign out. Its strings for all nine languages are in `_build/i18n_acct.py`. The header button is hidden below 600px, where the Menu and the footer carry the link instead.
- Admin page (`admin.html`, owner only): users, orders and payments with search and CSV export. Only the address in the Cloudflare setting `ADMIN_EMAIL` can open it; its strings are in `_build/i18n_admin.py`. New sign-ups are emailed to the owner.
- Services and prices (v3.8): the price list is `_build/catalog.py` — edit it there and run the build; the page, the order panel and the server all follow. Tapping a service opens an order panel (timing, length, brief, live price). Single-price services with ASAP or 2–4 weeks are ordered at once; everything else is a request the owner prices in the admin page (optionally in parts). Orders show on the account page; the order form also has “Send to my account”. Strings: `_build/i18n_shop.py`. Details: `_db/README.md`.
- UPI payments (v3.9): the order page shows a Pay button on phones and a QR code on computers (`assets/js/qr.js`), then takes the 12-digit UPI reference; the owner presses Mark paid or Reject in Admin, and the customer gets an email either way. The UPI ID and payee name are Cloudflare settings (`UPI_ID`, `UPI_ID_BACKUP`, `UPI_PAYEE_NAME`), not in this repo.
- Privacy (v3.10): `privacy.html` says what is kept and why (strings in `_build/i18n_privacy.py`; edit `PRIVACY` in `_build/build.py` and update `PRIVACY_UPDATED`). Signed-in people can ask to delete their data on the account page; the owner does it in Admin → Deletions. Old sign-in history is cleared after 90 days. Details: `_db/README.md`.
- Polish (v3.11): Riot refresh in orange and crimson after Aamir's references — an orange halo behind the sticker girl (now with a white scarf, orange headphones, one red and one blue eye, a wrapped katana; drawn in the page so she moves, `assets/img/chars/riot.svg`), gothic corner frames, falling ash, an engraved style letter and a crimson edge flash at S rank. Every shape is drawn for this site (no game logos or characters). Home has a role line, three featured works and “Currently building”; the order form is three steps (service, details, review and send); Work has a mission timeline and a small missions terminal (type `help`). Google gets Person and WebSite data on the home page; Inspirations sends 640px images to phones.
- 404 page (v3.11): the Sundown ride — ride Juniper home at sunset, jump fences, rocks, tumbleweeds and cacti, collect apples; best distance is kept in the browser. All drawn on a canvas in `assets/js/main.js` ("Sundown ride"). The lighthouse game is on `play.html`.
- Sundown Trail on Work (v3.11): an in-game film (`assets/media/sundown-trail*.{mp4,webp}`, made from Aamir's 4K "Timeline 1" at 1080p, no sound track because the original is silent) and eight clean screenshots in `assets/img/sundown/` (1280 px + 640 px copies), captions in `SUNDOWN_SHOTS` in `_build/build.py`.
