(02) The story so far

This site isn’t a résumé. It’s a save file.

Every chapter here is a checkpoint: what I could do, what I couldn’t yet, and what I was trying next. Some pages are finished. Most are still loading.

I study computer science at Tezpur University. Outside class I cut videos and animate type, build a western game called Sundown Trail, and write control code that keeps a robot from falling over. They look like different jobs. To me it’s one job: making things that move people.
