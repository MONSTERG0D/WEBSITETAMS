Mission 02

Sundown Trail

A western game, built one piece at a time

Red Dead Redemption 2 made me want to build a world, not just a level. Sundown Trail is my attempt: a frontier game with mid-poly visuals, a paper-journal interface and a lot of dust.

I’m building it one system at a time: terrain, movement, menus, then the story. The pause menu above is one of the first pieces I finished, and this site’s menu is modelled on it. Press Esc.

In development
Focus
Game design · UI · Blender environment art
Platform
Browser first, then Android & iOS

Mid-poly, 30 fps, baked Blender textures

Mission 03

Blender Studies

Every Blender artist starts with the donut. Mine came with a mug, a spoon and a beam of late-afternoon light, and I kept relighting it to learn how mood changes with one lamp. Then came a walnut table with wood-grain shading and a soft studio floor.

Mission 04

Echo Balancer

e-Yantra Robotics Competition 2026–27

The challenge is to keep an unstable robot upright. I’m working through the theme’s tasks in CoppeliaSim, starting with a PID controller and then moving to LQR for smoother, more efficient balancing.

It’s the most maths I’ve ever enjoyed: every gain I tune shows up right away as a wobble or as stillness.

Status
In progress
Stack
Python · CoppeliaSim
Control
PID → LQR
Competition
eYRC by e-Yantra, IIT Bombay

Mission 05

Arm Control Suite

Desktop software for my robotic arm

I have a physical robotic arm with a gripper. I’m writing desktop software that simulates it, works out its motion, shows it in 3D and then sends the same moves to the real arm.

The goal: test a move on screen, press one button and watch the real arm do it.

Status
In development
Hardware
Robotic arm + gripper
Firmware
Arduino IDE
Software
Simulation · kinematics · 3D view · control

Smart Bin

Smart India Hackathon 2026 — Team LUMOS

An IoT system for smart waste management. Bins measure how full they are and report over long-range radio, then a priority engine tells collection drivers which bins need them first.

I tend to make big plans and struggle to finish them. So I built a tracker for a 90-day challenge covering academics, programming, projects and skills, with small daily tasks and no overload.
