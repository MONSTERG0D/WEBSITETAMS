VIChapter VI — Side quests

Side quests welcome.

I take on a small number of freelance projects alongside my studies. If you have a story to tell, a product to show or a site that needs to exist, let’s talk.

  • 01

    Video editing

    Reels, ads, YouTube and short films: the cut, colour grade, sound and pacing. Tools: Premiere Pro, After Effects, DaVinci Resolve.

  • 02

    Motion graphics

    Kinetic typography, title sequences, logo animations and spec ads. See the motion reel.

  • 03

    Web design

    Layouts with a clear hierarchy and a little personality, designed mobile-first.

  • 04

    Web development

    Fast, hand-coded HTML, CSS and JavaScript sites like this one. No bloat, easy to host.

How it works

Four steps. No surprises.

Step 1

Brief

You tell me the goal, the audience and the deadline. I ask the annoying questions.

Step 2

Board

References, a rough storyboard or wireframe, and an agreed direction before I build anything.

Step 3

Build

I make it and share progress, with two rounds of revisions included.

Step 4

Deliver

Final files in the formats you need, and the project files if you want them.

The case

Why work with me. Three exhibits.

  • A

    Two languages

    I speak both design and code, so the thing you approve is the thing that ships.

  • B

    Obsessive about feel

    Years of games and films taught me that timing is everything. I sweat the frames.

  • C

    Honest

    Clear timelines, clear limits. If it isn’t my strength, I’ll tell you.
