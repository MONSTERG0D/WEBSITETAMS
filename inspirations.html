Everything I love leaves a mark.

These are the animals, games, films, anime and people I keep coming back to, along with the one thing I took from each. You’ll find small nods to most of them hidden around this site.

00 · The pack · always

Wolves.

“For the strength of the Pack is the Wolf, and the strength of the Wolf is the Pack.”Rudyard Kipling · The Law of the Jungle · 1894

My favourite animal. Wolves are loyal to their pack but still able to walk alone, and they’re patient, smart and loud when it matters. They’re why the footer says “run with the pack”.
