IChapter I — Origin

Every game starts with a tutorial. This is mine.

Welcome. This is where I keep a record of my work and my life as I go: what I can do, what I’m building and where I’m headed. It’s a log that keeps getting updated, not a finished résumé.

Why this exists

I wanted one place that shows the whole picture: the skills, the projects in progress, the failures and the people and stories that keep me going.

Most portfolios only show the highlight reel. I’d rather you see the run itself: the technologies I care about, the problems I’m stuck on and the goals I’ve set. If it does its job, you’ll leave knowing what I do and who I am.

Who I am

I’m Aamir Mehtab Sheikh, a B.Tech Computer Science & Engineering student at Tezpur University in Assam.

My toolkit is a strange mix: a sketchbook, a video timeline, Blender, a game engine and a C++ compiler. I like it that way. Each one is a different way of making something people feel. A cut that lands on the beat, a menu that feels good to scroll through and a robot that finally balances all give me the same small rush.

The run so far

Checkpoints.

  1. 2025

    Joined B.Tech CSE at Tezpur University

    New city, new syllabus, and the first time I took code seriously.

  2. Jan 2026

    Shipped version one of this site

    Hand-written HTML, a few pages and a lot of blue. It worked, and I learned I could put something on the internet.

  3. Jun – Aug 2026

    Went deep on motion design

    A Liquid Death spec spot, kinetic-type studies and my very first card-flip animation. I learned that text can perform.

  4. Sep 2026

    Smart India Hackathon + the 90-day challenge

    Built Smart Bin with Team LUMOS and started a 90-day plan to get better at academics, programming and shipping projects.

  5. Oct 2026

    Robots, a western and a 4K ad

    eYRC Echo Balancer, software for my robotic arm, Sundown Trail and the “Money Follows” edit. Then I rebuilt this site from scratch.
