IIChapter II — A survivor is born

Everyone shows the highlight reel. Here’s the rest.

These are the struggles I’m dealing with right now, and what I’m doing about each one. I’m still working through all of them.

01

Time

There are never enough hours.

College, competitions, editing, a game and a robot. I say yes to everything, then run out of day. Big plans are easy to make. Finishing them is the hard part.

Base camp

Small daily tasks instead of giant plans. My 90 Day Progress tracker keeps me honest, one finished thing at a time.

02

The learning curve

Too much to learn, all at once.

Control theory for eYRC, C++ from the basics, Blender, game engines, After Effects. Some weeks it feels like drinking from a fire hose.

Base camp

I learn by building. Each new idea goes into a real project the same week: a balancer, a game system or a tool.

03

Self-doubt

The voice that says I’m not good enough.

There are days I question whether I can actually pull any of this off, usually right before something works.

Base camp

I keep receipts. Every finished piece on this site is evidence against that voice.

04

Resources

Not always the right tools.

Hardware is expensive, renders are slow and my laptop has opinions about 4K footage.

Base camp

Free and open tools first: Blender, Godot, Krita, DaVinci Resolve, and simulators before hardware.

05

Balance

Too many tabs open in life.

Juggling studies, projects, competitions and people means something is always waiting.

Base camp

Clear goals, honest priorities and asking mentors for help instead of carrying everything alone.

Every version of me that failed taught the next one something.

— Note to self, build 17
